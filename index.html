Email Header Analyzer

Trace the delivery path in your browser

Paste the raw headers of an email and this tool reconstructs the delivery path from the Received chain, surfaces the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results, and flags mismatches that can indicate spoofing. This is a defensive and educational tool: it reads text only and nothing leaves this page. It does not fetch DNS or re-check any signature, it just reports what the headers say.

All parsing happens locally. The headers are never sent to any server.
